YouTube_OS Connector Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What this tool is

YouTube_OS Connector is a private tool used by one channel owner to read the data of their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it can access

With the permission of the signed-in Google account, the tool uses two read-only scopes: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It reads channel details, video lists and channel analytics such as views and watch time. It cannot upload, change or delete anything.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce performance reports for the owner of the channel.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are never published. Reports are stored on that computer and may be included in the owner's private backup. The owner may process reports with their own AI assistant and private dashboard.

Sharing

The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Removing access stops all further data reads.

Google and YouTube terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen of this app.
